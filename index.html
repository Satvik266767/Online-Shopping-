Customer Segmentation

K-Means Clustering (k=4)

High-Value Loyalists

Size: 23% (~276)

Characteristics: High spend ₹9,200, high frequency 11.4/mo, satisfaction 4.6, mostly 25–44, repeat buyers.

Strategy: VIP programs, early access, loyalty rewards.

Occasional Shoppers

Size: 31% (~372)

Characteristics: Moderate spend ₹3,800, low frequency 3.2/mo, satisfaction 3.8, mixed ages.

Strategy: Re-engagement campaigns, bundle offers.

At-Risk Churners

Size: 18% (~216)

Characteristics: Low spend ₹2,100, declining frequency 1.8/mo, low satisfaction 2.4, older users.

Strategy: Win-back offers, personalized outreach, feedback surveys.

Discount-Driven

Size: 28% (~336)

Characteristics: Moderate spend ₹4,400, high frequency 8.6/mo when discounts available, satisfaction 3.5.

Strategy: Flash sales, loyalty discounts, upsell premium categories.

Conclusions

Males exhibit significantly higher monthly spending compared to females, with an average difference of ₹680.

Order frequency is the strongest predictor of monthly spend, followed by age and satisfaction levels.

Amazon dominates platform preference, but age groups show distinct platform choices.

Discount users order more frequently, suggesting price sensitivity drives purchase behavior.

Four distinct customer segments identified, with high-value loyalists representing the most profitable group.
